How Do I Communicate?

This short survey helps you reflect on how you communicate with colleagues and students, including when team-teaching with an OTE.

How it works
Answer 20 statements. Your four scores and personal reflection tips will appear immediately when you finish.

Rating scale
1 — Not at all like me · 2 — A little like me · 3 — Sometimes like me · 4 — Mostly like me · 5 — Very much like me

There are no right or wrong answers. Everyone can use all four styles, and communication can change depending on the situation. This is an informal workshop tool inspired by DiSC, not an official or validated DiSC assessment.
